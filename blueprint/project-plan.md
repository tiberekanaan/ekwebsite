## 1. Problem - What problem are we solving?

Empower Kiribati is a locally-led NGO in Bikenibeu, South Tarawa (operating
name of Digital Kiribati Inc, registered 6 May 2024). It partners with
ministries, funders and community organisations to design, deliver and measure
programmes in leadership, digital participation, partnerships and innovation.

The website has three jobs:

- Show that the organisation is real, active and credible, so funders and
  partners can evaluate it (programmes, delivery record, figures, news).
- Give community groups practical resources, starting with grant-writing
  guides written for Kiribati.
- Let non-technical staff publish and edit all of this themselves in a CMS,
  with structured content types instead of free-form pages.

## 2. Users - Who is this for?

Readers:

- Prospective funders, embassies and ministries evaluating a partner.
- Existing partners and ministry counterparts checking what happened recently.
- Community organisations, churches, schools and youth looking for programmes,
  exchange opportunities and resources.
- Journalists and others looking for a contact or a recent story.

Editors: Empower Kiribati staff working in the Strapi admin. They are trusted
and non-technical.

## 3. Features - What does the MVP need?

Already shipped (see `build-plan.md` for the full checklist):

- Landing page built from CMS blocks: hero with latest-news line, featured
  programmes, What's new, track record, strategic focus, Why-we-exist teaser,
  partner logo marquee, pull quote, Work-with-us close.
- Programmes grouped by strategy, each with partners by role, a delivery
  record of dated activities, and testimonials.
- News (index, slugged stories), blog, events, resources with downloadable and
  video blocks, and long-form grant guides with tags, outline and related
  articles.
- About page with the nine-step Why-we-exist cycle, partners pages, contact
  form and newsletter sign-up stored in Strapi.
- Optional Kiribati (Gilbertese) translations on programmes, resources, news
  and blog.
- Site search (Pagefind), SEO (canonical domain guard, sitemap including
  on-request pages, NGO and NewsArticle structured data, OG images), Vercel
  Web Analytics.
- Every editorial string on the landing page, header and footer editable in
  Strapi.

## 4. Data - What are we storing?

Strapi 5 content types (`backend/src/api/`):

- Single types: `homepage` (dynamic zone of `blocks.*` components plus SEO
  fields), `global` (site name, logos, nav, footer, contact details, socials),
  `basic-page` (About), `partners-page`.
- Collections: `project` (displayed as Programme), `pillar` (displayed as
  Strategy), `activity`, `partner`, `testimonial`, `news-update`, `blog`,
  `event`, `resource`, `article`, `tag`.
- Submissions: `web-form` (contact form), `subscriber` (newsletter emails).

Programmes, resources, news and blog are localised (`en`, `en-KI`). Entries are
addressed by `documentId`; public URLs use title-derived slugs.

## 5. Tech - What stack are we using?

- Frontend: Astro 6 on the Velocity starter, Tailwind CSS v4 (CSS-first
  `@theme` tokens), TypeScript strict, Zod 4 via `astro/zod`, `marked` for
  rich text, Astro Actions for forms, Pagefind search, `@astrojs/vercel`
  adapter. Package manager: pnpm (`frontend/pnpm-lock.yaml`).
- Backend: Strapi 5.47 (TypeScript), SQLite locally, Strapi Cloud in
  production. Package manager: npm (`backend/package-lock.json`).
- Data flow: build-time Content Loader collections in
  `frontend/src/content.config.ts`; on-request (SSR) detail pages for
  translated content; idempotent `backend/apply-*.js` scripts for production
  content migrations.

## 7. UI/UX - How should this look and feel?

- The `ek` palette: deep teal `ek-900`, mist, paper, lime accents. Playfair
  Display headings, condensed uppercase eyebrows with the three-wave glyph,
  pill buttons, rounded photo cards.
- Sections alternate dark and light backgrounds; content reveals on scroll and
  respects reduced motion; no-JS visitors get a complete static page.
- Editorial voice: British spelling, no em dashes, plain and specific. Name
  South Tarawa, never "across Kiribati". No staff names or photos. People are
  quoted by role and month only, with an anonymity note.

## 8. Deployment - Where and how will this ship?

- Frontend: Vercel project `ekwebsite`, domain `empower.org.ki` with
  `www.empower.org.ki` redirecting to it. Build `pnpm build` (Astro build plus
  the Pagefind index). Env vars: `STRAPI_URL`, `STRAPI_API_TOKEN`, `SITE_URL`.
- Backend: Strapi Cloud
  (`https://determined-strength-17a6de9eef.strapiapp.com`), deployed on push to
  `main`; schema changes take about 15 minutes to deploy.
- Pushing `main` to GitHub deploys both. A Strapi publish, unpublish or delete
  webhook triggers a Vercel rebuild, so content changes go live in about a
  minute.
- Domain: registered with Mount Systems (renews 14 May 2027). DNS is on Vercel
  nameservers since 6 November 2026. Email is Google Workspace (MX, SPF, DKIM,
  DMARC records in Vercel DNS).

## 9. Usage model and constraints (optional)

- Public, internet-facing marketing and information site for a small NGO.
  No visitor accounts.
- Untrusted input reaches the backend only through the contact form and the
  newsletter sign-up (Zod validation, honeypot fields).
- Editors are trusted. Production API tokens are short-lived and deleted after
  use.
- Content about programme participants follows consent rules: role and month
  attribution only, partners named only with `consent_to_name`.
